Privacy Policy for Tiger Path Match

Effective Date: June 20, 2026

Tiger Path Match is a casual puzzle game developed for entertainment purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Tiger Path Match does not require users to create an account, log in, or provide personal information.

The app may store the following game-related data locally on your device:

  • Game progress
  • Level stars
  • Coins, gems, energy, and item counts
  • Collection unlocks
  • Sound settings

This data is used only to provide normal game features and is stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Payment card information
  • Account credentials

3. Local Storage

The app uses local device storage to save your game progress and settings. This information remains on your device unless you uninstall the app, clear app data, or reset progress inside the game.

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The current version of Tiger Path Match does not require a user account and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or social login services.

If future versions add online features, advertisements, analytics, cloud saves, or purchases, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Children’s Privacy

Tiger Path Match is designed as a casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties because the app does not intentionally collect personal information.

7. Data Security

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Please note that no method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the app is designed to avoid collecting sensitive personal information.

8. Your Choices

You may remove locally stored game data by:

  • Clearing the app data in Android system settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Using the in-game reset option where available

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a new effective date.
